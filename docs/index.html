Questions, answered.

Do customers need to install an app?

No. CardYeung runs inside Telegram as a mini app. Customers open the bot or scan your QR and their card is ready.

What does it cost?

Nothing for shops or customers. There are no subscriptions and no per-stamp fees.

How do I stamp a customer’s card?

Open Shop studio, tap Scan customer and point your phone at the QR on their card. You can also type the code under the QR.

What if a customer loses their Telegram account?

When they first open the wallet they get a recovery code. Entering it from a new Telegram account moves all their cards and rewards across.

Can I keep my existing membership cards?

Yes. Save any membership number in your wallet so it is with you at checkout.

Can someone fake stamps?

No. Only the owner of a program can award stamps or redeem rewards, and every action is checked against the owner’s Telegram account.
